Power BI Case Study

Real Estate Property Management Dashboard

From Raw Data to Actionable Insights: Transformed a raw housing dataset into a robust, star-schema data model in Power BI to provide a 360° view of portfolio health, renovation needs, and investment effectiveness.

Live Interactive Dashboard

The Problem

Real estate companies often struggle to make data-driven decisions due to the fragmented nature of their information. They need a single source of truth to understand their portfolio’s health — knowing which properties are performing well, which need renovation, and where investment is most effective across different locations and property types.

My Solution

  • Transformed raw housing data into a robust Star Schema data model.
  • Created clean Fact and Dimension tables (Location, Condition, Renovation Status, Bedrooms, Floors, Waterfront).
  • Engineered advanced DAX measures for key KPIs (Renovation %, Condition ratings, Regional property counts).
  • Designed an intuitive, multi-page dashboard delivering a 360° portfolio view.
