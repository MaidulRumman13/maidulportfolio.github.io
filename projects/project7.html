AirBnb Seattle Rental Market Analysis

Unveiling the secrets of Seattle's rental market through interactive data visualizations. This comprehensive dashboard analyzes average prices, bedroom availability, geographic trends, and yearly revenue patterns.

Interactive Tableau Dashboard

If the dashboard takes a moment to load, please ensure your browser allows third-party cookies for Tableau Public.

Project Objective

To explore Seattle's AirBnb rental market data and determine relationships between pricing, location, bedroom availability, and revenue patterns. This practice project demonstrates data exploration and visualization skills using Tableau.

Tools & Technologies

  • Tableau: Interactive dashboard creation with maps, bar charts, and line graphs
  • Excel: Data cleaning and preprocessing
  • Data Source: Kaggle AirBnb Seattle Dataset

Key Insights Explored

💰

Average Price Per Bedroom

Analysis reveals pricing trends from 1-bedroom ($96.2) to 6-bedroom ($584.8) properties, showing clear correlation between bedroom count and pricing.

📍

Geographic Price Distribution

Interactive map visualization showing price variations across different zip codes in Seattle, with hotspots identified in premium areas.

📈

Revenue Trends Over Time

Year-over-year revenue analysis showing growth patterns and seasonal trends in the AirBnb market throughout 2016.

🏠

Bedroom Availability

1-bedroom listings dominate the market (1,811 listings), while 6-bedroom properties are rare (only 5 listings).

💵

Zip Code Performance

Detailed breakdown of average prices by zip code, with 98119 leading at $200+ average, followed by other premium areas.

📊

Market Distribution

Comprehensive analysis of listing distribution, pricing strategies, and market saturation across different property types.
