Power BI Case Study

FMCG Sales & Promotion Performance Analysis

Leveraging data-driven strategies to uncover actionable opportunities for optimizing revenue and sales uplift. This analysis reveals how top stores succeed and which promotions deliver true ROI.

Live Interactive Dashboard

The Challenge

In the highly competitive FMCG domain, understanding store-level performance and the true effectiveness of promotional strategies is critical. The business needed to move beyond surface-level metrics to identify which stores were driving incremental revenue and which promotions were actually delivering sustainable ROI without eroding margins.

My Approach

  • Evaluated store-level performance to identify top and bottom performers.
  • Calculated the ROI of various promotional strategies (Discount vs. BOGOF vs. Cashback).
  • Analyzed product category dynamics to find correlations between promotion types and sales lift.
  • Built an interactive Power BI dashboard to enable hyper-targeted future campaigns.

Key Wins & Business Insights

🏪

Store Performance

Identified top 10 stores driving ₹207M Incremental Revenue (IR). Chennai stores like STCHE-7 led with an exceptional 142.66% IR%, revealing city-specific patterns ripe for replication.

🏷️

Promotion Impact

Discount-based promotions significantly outperformed BOGOF/cashback in driving IR. Striking the right balance yielded a 226K unit ISU lift and a ₹171.99M margin impact.

📦

Product Dynamics

Specific categories (e.g., Combo packs) showed remarkable sales lift during promotions, enabling hyper-targeted future marketing campaigns.

Why This Matters

This analysis goes beyond surface metrics. By revealing exactly how top stores succeed and which promotions deliver true ROI, businesses can confidently replicate success across underperforming stores and optimize promotional spend for maximum, sustainable impact.
