Omniretail Sales Performance Analysis

A comprehensive retail analytics project utilizing SQL, Excel Power Query, and Power BI to clean messy transactional data, uncover hidden trends, and drive actionable business growth.

Live Interactive Dashboard

The Challenge

Omniretail needed a unified view of their $497K+ revenue across multiple regions and product categories. They faced challenges with a 10% overall return rate (peaking at 15.63% in Electronics) and needed to identify top-performing stores, customer segments, and seasonal trends to optimize marketing and operational strategies.

My Approach

  • Cleaned messy transactional data using Excel Power Query.
  • Wrote 10 core + 9 ad-hoc SQL queries (utilizing CTEs, window functions, and date parsing).
  • Built an interactive Power BI dashboard with slicers, drill-downs & live KPIs.

SQL Data Pipeline & Code Highlights

Beyond visualization, I engineered the data layer using advanced SQL. Here are snippets of the data cleaning, CTEs, and business logic queries I wrote.

data_cleaning.sql 
-- Converting messy string dates to proper DATE format
ALTER TABLE Sales ADD COLUMN Sale_Date_New DATE;

UPDATE Sales
SET Sale_Date_New = STR_TO_DATE(Sale_Date, '%c/%e/%Y');

ALTER TABLE Sales DROP COLUMN Sale_Date;
ALTER TABLE Sales RENAME COLUMN Sale_Date_New TO Sale_Date;
advanced_analytics.sql 
-- Finding store with highest sales count but lowest revenue
WITH StoreStats AS (
    SELECT Store_ID,
           ROUND(SUM(Total_Sale_Amount), 2) AS TotalRevenue,
           COUNT(Sale_ID) AS NumberOfSales,
           RANK() OVER (ORDER BY COUNT(Sale_ID) DESC) AS SalesRank,
           RANK() OVER (ORDER BY SUM(Total_Sale_Amount) ASC) AS RevenueRank
    FROM Sales GROUP BY Store_ID
)
SELECT * FROM StoreStats ORDER BY SalesRank ASC LIMIT 1;
ad_hoc_business_logic.sql 
-- Store Performance Efficiency (Revenue per Sale) & Category Return Rates
SELECT Store_ID, 
       ROUND(SUM(Total_Sale_Amount) / COUNT(DISTINCT Sale_ID), 2) AS Revenue_Per_Sale
FROM Sales GROUP BY Store_ID ORDER BY Revenue_Per_Sale DESC;

SELECT Product_Category,
       ROUND(SUM(CASE WHEN Returned = 'Yes' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) * 100.0 / COUNT(*), 2) AS ReturnPercentage
FROM Sales GROUP BY Product_Category ORDER BY ReturnPercentage DESC;

Key Business Insights

🏆

Top Performing Region

West region generated the highest total sales ($0.14M), indicating stronger customer engagement.

⚠️

Quality Red Flag

Electronics category shows a 15.63% return rate, suggesting potential quality or satisfaction issues.

📅

Peak Sales Quarters

Q2 and Q3 are the best-performing quarters, likely driven by mid-year shopping events.

💳

Payment Preferences

Credit Card and Net Banking together form over 50% of total revenue.

👥

Customer Concentration

Top 10 customers contribute 25%+ of total sales, highlighting the value of loyalty.

📈

Ad-hoc Bonus Findings

South region has the highest AOV ($1,063.78). Store S008 is the most efficient ($1,148/sale).

Strategic Recommendations

Scale the West Model

Expand successful marketing campaigns from the West region and replicate the model in lower-performing North & East regions.

Fix Electronics Quality

Investigate supplier issues and enhance customer feedback channels to reduce the 15.63% return rate.

Launch Q2–Q3 Promotions

Capitalize on peak shopping quarters with focused, data-backed promotional campaigns.

Reward Top-Spending Customers

Introduce loyalty or cashback programs for high-value customers (e.g., C0001, C0113) to drive retention.
