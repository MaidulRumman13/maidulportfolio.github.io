## 🔍 What You'll Find in My Projects

My analytical workflow typically includes:

1. **Problem Definition** — Understand the business question and analytical objectives.
2. **Data Exploration** — Inspect datasets, identify data-quality issues, and understand key variables.
3. **Data Cleaning & Transformation** — Handle missing values, standardize formats, and prepare analysis-ready data.
4. **Data Analysis** — Apply SQL, Python, Excel, or statistical methods to investigate trends and relationships.
5. **Visualization & Reporting** — Build dashboards and visual summaries to communicate findings.
6. **Insights & Recommendations** — Translate analytical results into practical, evidence-based recommendations.

The exact workflow and methods vary depending on each project's objectives and dataset.
